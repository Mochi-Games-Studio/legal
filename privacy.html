Brainy Kids - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Brainy Kids does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All game data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account. The only thing we ever receive is fully anonymous game ratings (👍/👎) when you choose to send them — see "Anonymous Game Ratings" below.

Overview

Brainy Kids is a brain training app designed for all ages. We take privacy seriously, especially because our app is used by children. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Do Not Collect

Brainy Kids does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not use third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not track location or device identifiers.
  • We do not associate any data we receive with a specific user, device, or profile.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Player profiles: A display name, avatar emoji or photo, and month/year of birth that you choose during setup. This information never leaves your device (or your private iCloud account if you enable sync).
  • Game progress: Scores, experience points, completed challenges, earned badges, and brain skill levels.
  • Preferences: Theme selection, sound settings, and difficulty feedback.

All of this data is stored using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults) and is not accessible to us or any third party.

iCloud Sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your game progress and profiles are stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is:

  • Stored in your personal, private iCloud container.
  • Only accessible by you, on devices signed in to your iCloud account.
  • Not accessible to us or any third party.
  • Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

iCloud sync is optional and can be disabled in the app's Settings.

Anonymous Game Ratings

Each game's menu screen has an optional 👍 / 👎 button so you can tell us whether you enjoy it. If — and only if — you tap one of these, the app sends us:

  • The name of the game (e.g. "Sudoku")
  • Whether you tapped 👍 or 👎
  • The version of the app you are using
  • The date and time of the rating

That is the entire payload. We never receive your name, your profile, your device ID, your iCloud ID, your IP address (beyond what Apple's CloudKit infrastructure handles transparently), or anything else that could identify you. We use only Apple's first-party CloudKit framework — no third-party server, SDK, or analytics product is involved. The data is stored in our own CloudKit Public Database and we look at it in aggregate to decide which games to invest in or retire. You can opt out of every game's rating simply by not tapping the buttons.

Camera and Photo Library

The app offers optional features that use the camera and photo library:

  • Profile photo: You may optionally take or choose a photo as your profile avatar. This photo is stored locally on your device (and in your private iCloud if sync is enabled). It is not uploaded anywhere else.
  • Celebration photos: After completing daily challenges, you may take a celebratory photo. This photo is saved only to your device's photo library. It is not uploaded or shared by the app.

Camera and photo library access require your explicit permission via iOS system prompts. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Children's Privacy

Brainy Kids is designed to be safe for children of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations:

  • We do not collect personal information from children (or anyone).
  • We do not include advertising or in-app purchases.
  • We do not include links to external websites or social media within the app experience.
  • We do not enable children to share information publicly.
  • All data remains on the device or in the family's private iCloud account.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time:

  • Individual profiles can be deleted from the profile selection screen (long-press a profile).
  • All data can be erased from the Settings screen using the "Reset All Data" option.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
